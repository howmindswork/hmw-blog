What are cord-cutting rituals?

Cord-cutting rituals release emotional ties. They help you let go of grief

These rituals are part of The Emotional Completion Ritual methodology. They promote healing and closure

Research shows that ritual practices can reduce grief symptoms (Source: PubMed, 2018)

How do I practice cord-cutting?

Start with a calm space. Light a candle to create a soothing atmosphere

You can use a simple cord-cutting ritual: 1. Set intention, 2. Visualize the cord, 3. Cut the cord
