Frequently Asked Questions

Is it normal to not cry when grieving?

Yes, it's normal to not cry when grieving. Everyone grieves differently.

How long does grief last?

Grief can last for months or even years. It's a unique process for each person.

What is the Emotional Completion Ritual?

The Emotional Completion Ritual is a 3-step process to acknowledge, release, and integrate emotions during grief.

Can I grieve without rituals?

Yes, you can grieve without rituals. However, rituals like the Star Feeding Ritual can provide structure and support.

How do I know if I'm grieving healthily?

Healthy grieving involves acknowledging your emotions and taking steps to release and integrate them. Learn more about healthy grief.

What if I'm still struggling with grief?

If you're still struggling with grief, consider seeking support from a therapist or support group. You can also try the Emotional Completion Protocol.

Are there any online resources for grief support?

Yes, there are many online resources for grief support, including NIMH and PubMed.
