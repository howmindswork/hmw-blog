Grief In The Body

Note: This content is not a substitute for professional therapy or medical care. If you are in crisis, please reach out to a mental health professional.

Grief feels like a heavy weight in your body. It's a physical sensation that can be overwhelming.

Key Takeaways

  • Grief can cause physical symptoms like tension and pain
  • The body stores emotional trauma and grief
  • Releasing grief requires acknowledging and processing physical sensations
