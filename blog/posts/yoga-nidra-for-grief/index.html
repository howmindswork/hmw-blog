Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between yoga nidra and regular meditation?

Yoga nidra is a guided meditation that induces deep relaxation, while regular meditation can be more awareness-focused.

Can I practice yoga nidra at home?

Yes, you can practice yoga nidra at home with a guided audio recording.

How long does it take to see results from yoga nidra?

Results from yoga nidra can be seen in as little as one session, but consistent practice is recommended.

Is yoga nidra suitable for everyone?

Yoga nidra is generally suitable for everyone, but it's recommended to consult a healthcare professional before starting a new practice, especially if you have any underlying health conditions.
