Is emotional numbness a normal response to bereavement?

Yes, emotional numbness is a common response to bereavement, it's a way of coping with the pain.

The duration of emotional numbness varies, it can last from a few weeks to several months or even years.

While it's possible to heal on your own, seeking support from a therapist or a trusted practice like The Emotional Completion Ritual can be helpful.
