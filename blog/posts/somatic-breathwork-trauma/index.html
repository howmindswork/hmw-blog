Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between somatic breathwork and regular breathing exercises?

Somatic breathwork combines conscious breathing with physical awareness to release stored trauma, whereas regular breathing exercises focus on relaxation and stress relief

Can I practice somatic breathwork on my own?

Yes, you can practice somatic breathwork on your own, but it's recommended to start with guided sessions and gradually move to independent practice

Is somatic breathwork suitable for everyone?

Most people can practice somatic breathwork, but it's essential to consult with a healthcare professional if you have any underlying medical conditions

How long does it take to see results from somatic breathwork?

Results can vary, but many people experience reduced anxiety and emotional calm within a few weeks of regular practice, according to a study by the American Psychological Association
