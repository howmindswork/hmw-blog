Somatic practices release physical tension related to grief. This is backed by research from the University of California, Irvine.

Can grief rituals be done alone?

Yes, grief rituals can be done alone. In fact, a study by the National Institute of Mental Health found that solo rituals can be just as effective as group rituals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best grief ritual for me?

The best grief ritual for you will depend on your personal preferences and needs. You may want to try a few different rituals to see what works best for you.

How long does it take to heal from grief?

Healing from grief is a unique and individual process, and it can take time. Be patient with yourself and allow yourself to feel your emotions.

Can grief rituals be used for any type of loss?

Yes, grief rituals can be used for any type of loss, including the loss of a loved one, a relationship, or a significant life change.

How can I find support for my grief?

You can find support for your grief by talking to a trusted friend or family member, joining a support group, or seeking out professional counseling.
