Why does time bury stress instead of healing it?

You have probably been told that time heals. For an unfinished nervous system signal, that is closer to backwards. Time does not complete the loop. It packs more layers on top of it. Each new stressful week adds another sediment layer over the original charge, so the signal gets quieter and harder to reach, but it never actually switches off.

This is the core of the science the program is built on. Researchers like Bessel van der Kolk and Peter Levine have shown that the body keeps the score of incomplete stress and trauma. The response lives in muscle tension, held breath, and a guarded posture, not in your conscious memory. That is why you can know exactly why you feel awful and still feel awful. Insight lives in the part of you that reads English. The signal lives in the part that does not.

So managing it never finishes it. A breathing exercise can turn the volume down for an hour. A distraction can buy you an evening. But the moment the distraction ends, the signal is still on, so the rumination comes back at 2am right on schedule. Finishing is a different action than managing.
